# Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to our Help Center. Find answers to the most common questions below.

---

## Password Reset

Forgot your password? You can reset it in a few easy steps:

1. Go to the login page and click **"Forgot Password"**.
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your inbox for a reset link (valid for 15 minutes).
4. Click the link and set a new password.

If you don't receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact support.

---

## App Crash or Troubleshooting

If the app is crashing or behaving unexpectedly:

1. **Force-close** the app and reopen it.
2. **Check for updates** — an outdated version may cause issues.
3. **Clear the cache**: Settings → App Info → Clear Cache.
4. **Restart your device** to free up memory.
5. If the problem persists, uninstall and reinstall the app.

For persistent issues, please contact support with your device model and OS version.

---

## Payment Failed

If your payment did not go through:

- Verify that your card details (number, expiry, CVV) are correct.
- Ensure your billing address matches the one on file with your bank.
- Check that you have sufficient funds or credit available.
- Some banks block international or online transactions — contact your bank to authorise the charge.
- Try a different payment method (UPI, net banking, or another card).

If the amount was deducted but the order was not placed, it will be automatically refunded within 5–7 business days.

## Delivery Timelines

Standard delivery timelines:

| Delivery Type       | Estimated Time   |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Standard Shipping   | 5–7 business days |
| Express Shipping    | 2–3 business days |
| Same-Day Delivery   | By end of day (select cities) |

Tracking information is shared via email and SMS once your order is shipped. Delays may occur during public holidays or bad weather conditions.

## Refund Policy

We offer refunds within **14 days of the delivery date**.

- Refund requests must be raised within 14 days of receiving your order.
- The 14-day window starts from the **delivered date** (not the order date).
- Refunds are processed within 7–10 business days after approval.
- Refunds are credited back to the original payment method.
- Items must be unused, in original packaging, with tags intact.
- High-value orders (above ₹20,000) may require additional verification.

For orders delivered more than 14 days ago, we are unable to process refunds.

---

## Return Window

You can return most items within **14 days of delivery**.

- Log in to your account → Orders → Select order → Click **Return**.
- Choose a reason for the return.
- Schedule a pickup at your doorstep (available in most cities) or drop it at a partner store.
- Refunds are issued once the returned item passes quality inspection.

**Non-returnable items**: perishables, digital goods, personalised products, and hygiene items.

---

## Change Delivery Address

You can change your delivery address **before the order is shipped**:

1. Go to **My Orders** and select the order.
2. Click **Edit Address** (available only while status is "Processing").
3. Enter the new address and confirm.

Once an order has been shipped, the address cannot be changed. Contact the courier partner directly with your tracking ID for special assistance.

---

## Invoice Download

To download an invoice for your order:

1. Log in and go to **My Orders**.
2. Click on the specific order.
3. Scroll down and click **Download Invoice** (PDF format).

Invoices are generated automatically once an order is confirmed. For GST invoices or B2B billing, contact our support team with your GSTIN.

---

## Account Deletion

To permanently delete your account:

1. Go to **Profile → Settings → Account**.
2. Click **Delete Account** and confirm with your password.
3. Your account and personal data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

**Note**: Active orders must be completed or cancelled before account deletion. Pending refunds will still be processed to the original payment method.

---

## Coupon and Promo Code Issues

If your coupon or promo code is not working:

- Check the **expiry date** of the code — expired codes cannot be reactivated.
- Ensure the code applies to the items in your cart (some codes exclude sale items or specific categories).
- Verify the **minimum order value** requirement is met.
- Codes are usually **single-use** — if used previously, they won't work again.
- Copy-paste the code carefully to avoid typos or extra spaces.

If your code is valid and still not working, contact support with a screenshot of the error.
